Where it did not reproduce

Two differences, neither of them a broken result

Step latency: 219 µs → 525 µs

The authors' own UNREPRODUCIBLE.md marks this column machine-specific. A 2.4× gap on a laptop CPU is the expected outcome; "sub-millisecond per step" survives, the exact figure does not transfer.

Coverage: 46% → 41.5%

Not a code difference. The numerator is identical (113), the denominator grew from 244 to 272 as traces were added; healthy rose 2,080 → 2,765. The onset count with no denominator — 215/218 — matches exactly.

Everything else, including fit time (9.08 s vs 8.7 s) and footprint (3.95 MB), is within 5% or exact. The repo's own claim ledger passes 121/121.

Honest accounting7 / 11
The modification

A repeated call is its own reference

“Plausible-value corruption — a wrong-but-well-formed number — is undetectable from telemetry by construction and needs an external reference.”

The rule

Same tool, same canonical arguments, called twice: a deterministic tool must answer the same monetary figures. Two identical calls that disagree mean one result is not the tool's.

One function in derail/verify/checks.py, wired in as a fourth check. No threshold, no null, no calibration.

Why only money

Whole-string comparison false-alarmed on 28 / 2,765 healthy runs — truncated snippets, reworded 429s. Numeric tokens still cost 26. Monetary figures: 0.

The narrowing is the contribution, not a detail.

Gaps

What a laptop with no keys cannot claim

Not re-collected

Live repair rollouts, organic trace collection and live judge calibration need local models plus a Gemini key. Their arithmetic is verified from persisted traces; their sampling is not.

Not delivered by this environment

Pushing the repository to GitHub and recording a 15-minute walkthrough need credentials and a screen recorder. The local repo carries the commits; this deck is the recording's stand-in.

With both, the next step is the obvious one: re-run the repair table live and check whether 45% holds on fresh samples rather than on the authors' persisted ones.

Limits10 / 11
Takeaway

What I would tell someone deciding whether to trust this paper

  • The numbers are real: episode-level counts, not rounded percentages, across served, provoked, held-out and cross-model arms.
  • The paper is honest about its weak class — and that weak class is partly closable with an invariant the paper already had the data for.
  • The cost claim is the only thing that does not travel; everything else is hardware-independent and reproduces on a laptop in 45 minutes.
